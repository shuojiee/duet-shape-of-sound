# User Guide: Duet

## 🎶 The Concept
**Duet** is an interactive, two-player musical instrument designed for non-verbal conversation. By rolling colored marbles down a split-track system, players trigger melodic responses. The instrument is a dialogue: one person "speaks" with a marble, and the other "responds."

## 🚀 How to Use (Audio-Guided Startup)
When you power on the instrument, it will guide you through a calibration phase using a series of tones. **Follow these steps precisely to ensure the instrument can "see" your marbles correctly.**

| Step | Audio Cue | Action Required |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |
| 1 | **3-Note Melody** | System is starting up. |
| 2 | *5s Silence* | Prepare your white marbles. |
| 3 | **2-Note Tone** | **Place a White Marble** in front of every sensor. |
| 4 | *10s Silence* | Wait for the sensors to prepare. |
| 5 | **1-Note Tone** | **Do not touch.** System is recording White values. |
| 6 | *1.5s Silence* | Preparing for next step. |
| 7 | **2-Note Tone** | **Remove White Marbles** (Leave gates empty or use Black). |
| 8 | *10s Silence* | Wait for the sensors to prepare. |
| 9 | **1-Note Tone** | **Do not touch.** System is recording Black values. |
| 10 | *1.5s Silence* | Processing thresholds. |
| 11 | **3-Note Melody** | **Calibration Complete.** You may now begin the duet! |

## ⚖️ Troubleshooting
If the colors are not triggering correctly during play, restart the device and repeat the calibration sequence. Ensure that no bright sunlight is hitting the sensors directly.
